# Privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026. Publisher: b1rdmania.

field-kit has no publisher-run server, analytics or separate data store. The publisher does not receive your data through the plugin.
Your AI host processes prompts, company briefs, target accounts, guest lists and results under its privacy policy and settings.

The search script sends queries and page URLs to Exa or Perplexity, using your own API key.
Queries may include company names, locations and research questions. The script does not upload whole guest lists or output folders.
Each provider receives its own key for authentication. If the script cannot run, research uses your host's search providers.
Those services apply their own privacy policies and retention rules.

field-kit saves output only in your working folder's `field/` directory. Files remain there until you delete them.
Your host and search providers may retain conversations, files and requests. Use their controls to manage or delete that data.

For privacy questions, use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/b1rdmania/field-kit/issues). Issues are public; do not post personal data or keys.
