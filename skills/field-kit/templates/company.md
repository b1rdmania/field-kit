# Company context

Every field-kit workflow reads this file. Short answers are fine.

## What we sell

One or two sentences. What the product does and for whom.

## Who buys

The company types that buy. The strongest fit first.

## Who to meet

Job titles of the people who choose or use the product.

## Customers we can name

Public customers only, one per line, with what each one does.

## Competitors

One per line.

## Region and budget

The region for this plan. The field budget for the next 90 days, if known.

## What a win is

How sales counts an event as a success: meetings booked, opportunities, pipeline sourced or influenced. Use the sales team's own words.

## What not to claim

Pricing, roadmap, certifications or customers that must not appear in any output.
