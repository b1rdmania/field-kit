# Brief

Every field-kit workflow reads this file. field-kit drafts it from your website. Mark guesses "(guess)".

## What we sell

One or two sentences. What the product does and for whom.

## Who buys

The company types that buy. The strongest fit first.

## Who to meet

Job titles of the people who choose or use the product.

## Customers we can name

Public customers only, one per line, with what each one does.

## Competitors

One per line.

## Stage

Funding stage, team size, and who will run events.

## Region and budget

Where the events happen. What you can spend in the next 13 weeks.

## Average deal size

The usual first-year contract value. Leave blank if unknown.

## Typical costs

What a dinner, a demo night and a conference sponsorship cost you. Leave blank if unknown.

## What a win is

How sales counts an event as a success: meetings booked, opportunities, pipeline sourced or influenced. Use the sales team's own words.

## Past events

What you have done and what came of it.

## What not to claim

Pricing, roadmap, certifications or customers that must not appear in any output.

## The problem

field-kit fills this in: which problems from its list the company has, with the evidence.
