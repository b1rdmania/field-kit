# Brief

Larkspur is a made-up company for this example.

## What we sell

An API that turns PDFs and scans into structured data for AI products.

## Who buys

AI-native software companies first. Then legal and financial research tools, and enterprises that build internal document agents.

## Who to meet

CTO, VP or Head of Engineering, Head of AI, the product lead for a document feature.

## Customers we can name

None yet. Use the target accounts as seeds.

## Competitors

Reducto, Unstructured, LlamaParse, Mistral OCR

## Stage

Seed, 14 people. One marketer runs events with the founders.

## Region and budget

London and Western Europe. £60,000 for 13 weeks.

## Average deal size

£30,000 first-year contract.

## Typical costs

Dinner for 12: £2,500. Co-hosted demo night: £1,500. Conference sponsorship: £8,000 to £20,000.

## What a win is

Meetings booked with target accounts, and opportunities sales accepts within 30 days of an event.

## Past events

One booth at a London AI conference. 200 badge scans, no meetings that anyone tracked.

## What not to claim

Pricing, certifications, or any customer.

## The problem

Big sponsorships that produce badge scans and nothing else. No list of who to meet.
